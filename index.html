Running's a team sport now

Split Squads turns your solo runs into team relay races. Join a squad of four, each run a 2km leg, and your combined time takes on a rival squad in a weekly match.

You don't need to be in the same place or run at the same time. Run your leg whenever and wherever suits you, and the app does the rest.

How it works

  1. Create a squad and share the code with three friends, or join a squad they made.
  2. Everyone does a 2km rating run to set their baseline.
  3. Your squad gets matched each week against a squad of similar ability.
  4. Each runner completes a 2km leg, tracked by GPS.
  5. The fastest combined time wins, and you climb your local league.

A rating that means something

Your rating is your predicted 2km time. Every leg you race moves it. Run consistently and it settles. Pull off an upset and it moves to match.
