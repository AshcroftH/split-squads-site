Frequently asked questions

How does a match work?
Each week your squad of four is matched against another squad of similar ability. Every runner completes one 2km leg, in their own time and wherever they like. When all the legs are in, the squad with the fastest combined time wins.
How do I join my friends' squad?
Ask them for their squad code or invite link. Open the invite link on your iPhone, or enter the code under "Join with a code". A squad needs four runners before it can be matched.
What is the rating run?
It's a 2km run that sets your starting rating. Your rating is your predicted 2km time. Squads are matched on rating, so everyone in the squad needs one before you can find a match. After that, every leg you race updates it.
Why does the app need my location?
GPS is how the app measures the distance and time of your run. Allow location "While Using the App". Once you start a run it keeps measuring when your phone is locked or in your pocket, and iOS shows the blue location indicator while it does. Location is only used while you're recording a run. Your route stays on your phone, and only the distance, time and start time are uploaded.
My run didn't record properly. What should I do?
Make sure location is set to "While Using the App" and Precise Location is on (Settings → Split Squads → Location). Start the run outdoors with a clear view of the sky. Runs faster than 3:00/km are rejected as GPS drift. If something still looks wrong, email us with the date and time of the run.
How do I report another user?
In squad or match chat, tap the 🚩 report button, choose the person and the message, and pick a reason. Reports are reviewed, and we may remove content or accounts that break the rules. You can also email us.
How do I block someone?
In squad or match chat, tap 🚩 and then Block next to their name. You won't see their messages or get chat notifications from them, and they aren't told. To unblock, go to Me → Blocked runners.
How do I delete my account?
In the app, go to the Me tab and tap Delete account, then confirm with Apple. Your account, runs, rating and messages are deleted straight away, and Split Squads' access to your Apple ID is revoked. Legs you've already run stay in match results as "Deleted runner". If you're in a match and haven't run your leg yet, your squad forfeits that match.

If you can't open the app, email hello@leftform.co.uk and we'll delete it for you. See the privacy policy for details.
