Frequently asked questions

How does a match work?
Each week your squad of four is matched against another squad of similar ability. Every runner completes one 2km leg, in their own time and wherever they like. When all the legs are in, the squad with the fastest combined time wins.
How do I join my friends' squad?
Ask them for their squad code or invite link. Open the invite link on your iPhone, or enter the code under "Join with a code". A squad needs four runners before it can be matched.
What is the rating run?
It's a 2km run that sets your starting rating. Your rating is your predicted 2km time. Squads are matched on rating, so everyone in the squad needs one before you can find a match. After that, every leg you race updates it.
Why does the app need my location, including "Always"?
GPS is how the app measures the distance and time of your run. "Always" lets it keep measuring when your phone is locked or in your pocket. Location is only used while you're recording a run. Your route stays on your phone, and only the distance, time and start time are uploaded.
My run didn't record properly. What should I do?
Make sure location is set to "Always" and Precise Location is on (Settings → Split Squads → Location). Start the run outdoors with a clear view of the sky. Runs faster than 3:00/km are rejected as GPS drift. If something still looks wrong, email us with the date and time of the run.
How do I report another user?
You can report messages or users from inside the app. Reports are reviewed, and we may remove content or accounts that break the rules. You can also email us.
How do I delete my account?
Email hello@leftform.co.uk from the address linked to your account, or include your display name, and we'll delete your account and its data. In-app deletion is coming soon. See the privacy policy for details.
