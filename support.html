Frequently asked questions

How does a match work?
Every Monday your squad is matched with the closest squad in your league division. Every runner completes one 2km leg, in their own time and wherever they like, by Sunday at midnight. The squad with the fastest combined time wins. A squad that doesn't get all four legs in by the deadline loses the match. One run counts for every match your squad has that week.
How do leagues work?
Join your area league with a tap once all four runners have a rating. Each league has 10 divisions, and you start in the one that matches your squad's predicted time (Division 1 is the fastest). A season is 8 weekly matches: a win is 3 points, a dead heat 1. Reach 15 points to be promoted, 21 to win your division, and finish under 9 and you go down. Squads that join part-way through have lower targets and can't be relegated that season. A squad can be in one area league at a time, plus any company or brand leagues it's invited to.
Can a squad just run its rating runs slowly to get an easy division?
It won't help. Ratings update after every leg, and each week we check whether a squad has outgrown its division. A squad that's clearly too quick moves up straight away, starts on zero points in the new division, and is safe from relegation for the rest of that season.
What are challenges?
Friendly races with any squad, any time: one you know, one you find by name, or one near your pace that we suggest. You can also share a challenge link. The other squad has 3 days to accept, then both squads have 7 days to run. Challenges don't count for league points, but legs still move your ratings. Your squad can have one challenge on at a time.
Can I follow my friends' squads?
Yes. Open any squad from a league table and tap the star. Squads you follow appear under Favourites at the top of the Leagues tab, with their position and this week's match, and you'll get a notification when their match finishes. Tap the star again to stop following.
Can I choose which notifications I get?
Yes. In Me → Settings → Notifications you can switch each kind on or off: your matches, reminders, squad chat, challenges, favourites and league news. Switched-off notifications aren't sent at all. To stop them all, turn notifications off for Split Squads in iOS Settings.
What does the voice coach do?
While you run your leg it tells you your time and pace every 500 m, gives you a shout for the last 500 m, and calls your finish, even with your phone locked. Your music keeps playing and just dips while it speaks. "Coach" style also tells you how you compare with your last leg and what your squad needs to win. Change the style, choose Isabella or George, play a sample, or turn it off in Me → Settings → Voice coach.
How do I join my friends' squad?
Ask them for their squad code or invite link. Open the invite link on your iPhone, or enter the code under "Join with a code". A squad needs four runners before it can be matched.
What is the rating run?
It's a 2km run that sets your starting rating. Your rating is your predicted 2km time. Divisions and matches are based on ratings, so everyone in the squad needs one before your squad can join a league. After that, every leg you race updates it.
Why does the app need my location?
GPS is how the app measures the distance and time of your run. Allow location "While Using the App". Once you start a run it keeps measuring when your phone is locked or in your pocket, and iOS shows the blue location indicator while it does. Location is only used while you're recording a run. Your route stays on your phone, and only the distance, time and start time are uploaded.
My run didn't record properly. What should I do?
Make sure location is set to "While Using the App" and Precise Location is on (Settings → Split Squads → Location). Start the run outdoors with a clear view of the sky. Runs faster than 3:00/km are rejected as GPS drift. If something still looks wrong, email us with the date and time of the run.
How do I report another user?
In squad chat, tap the flag button, choose the person and the message, and pick a reason. Reports are reviewed, and we may remove content or accounts that break the rules. You can also email us.
How do I block someone?
In squad chat, tap the flag button and then Block next to their name. You won't see their messages or get chat notifications from them, and they aren't told. To unblock, go to Me → Settings → Blocked runners.
How do I delete my account?
In the app, go to Me, tap the gear for Settings, then Delete account and confirm. You don't need to sign in again. Your account, runs, rating and messages are deleted straight away. Leg times you've already run stay in match results as "Deleted runner". You can also email us and we'll do it for you.
