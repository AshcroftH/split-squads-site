Who we are

Split Squads is made by Left Form Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17494164). We are the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact us at hello@leftform.co.uk.

What we collect

Your account

  • Sign in with Apple: a unique identifier from Apple, plus your name and email address if you choose to share them. If you use "Hide My Email", we only see Apple's relay address. We also keep a token Apple gives us at sign-in, stored encrypted, used only to disconnect your Apple ID from Split Squads when you delete your account (so you don't have to sign in again to delete).
  • Display name: the name you choose, which other users can see.

Your runs

  • For each run you record: the distance, the elapsed time, when it started, and your time at each 500 m (so the voice coach can compare you with your last leg).
  • Your rating (predicted 2km time), your rating history and your best times.
  • We do not upload your GPS route. Your location is processed on your phone to measure the run, and only the totals above are sent to us.

Squads and matches

  • The squads and leagues you belong to, your division and league results, challenges you send or accept, your match results and leg times.
  • Messages you send in squad chat (and in match chat, which earlier versions of the app had).
  • Reports you make about other users or content, and reports made about you.
  • Runners you've blocked.

Your device

  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications, so we can tell you about matches and messages.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data.

Location

The app uses your location only while you are recording a run. Once you start a run it keeps working in the background, with the blue location indicator showing, so a run is still measured when your phone is locked. Location is not used at any other time, and your route never leaves your phone.

The voice coach plays recordings that come with the app (if one is missing it uses your iPhone's built-in voice). Nothing is sent anywhere and nothing is recorded: the app doesn't use your microphone.

What other users can see

Your display name, rating, squad, run times and match results are visible to other Split Squads users, for example on squad pages, match pages and league tables. Chat messages can be seen by the members of that chat (your squad, or both squads in a match).

Why we use it

  • To run the service: your account, matchmaking, results, leagues and chat (the legal basis is contract).
  • To keep the community safe: reviewing reports and stopping cheating, such as impossible run times (the legal basis is legitimate interests).
  • To send push notifications you've allowed (the legal basis is consent, which you can withdraw in iOS Settings).

Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf and only as needed:

  • Cloudflare: hosts our API.
  • Neon: hosts our database.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and push notification delivery.
  • Expo: routes push notifications to Apple.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use safeguards recognised under UK data protection law, such as the International Data Transfer Addendum or Standard Contractual Clauses. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Me → Settings → Delete account). When you do, we immediately delete your name, email, Apple sign-in link and token, push token, runs, rating history and chat messages, and revoke Split Squads' access to your Apple ID. Leg times you ran in matches stay in match results and league tables, shown as "Deleted runner", with nothing that identifies you. Backups held by our database provider are overwritten within 30 days.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, and you can object to or restrict how we use it. You can delete your account yourself in the app. For any other request, email hello@leftform.co.uk. We'll respond within one month.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Split Squads is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update it here and change the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we'll let you know in the app.
